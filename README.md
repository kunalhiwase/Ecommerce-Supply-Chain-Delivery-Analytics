# E-Commerce Supply Chain Delivery Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the supply chain operations of a global e-commerce business to identify delivery inefficiencies, profitability risks, and operational bottlenecks.

The objective is to understand why orders are delivered late, identify the biggest contributors to fulfillment failures, and recommend data-driven business improvements.

---

## Business Problem

Late deliveries directly impact customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and profitability.

The analysis focuses on answering:

- How severe is the late delivery problem?
- Which operational areas contribute most to delays?
- Are delays caused by specific regions, products, or shipping methods?
- How does delivery performance affect profitability?
- Can risky orders be identified before failure?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains historical e-commerce supply chain transactions including:

- Customer information
- Order details
- Shipping performance
- Product categories
- Geographic regions
- Sales and profit information

Total Records Analyzed:

**170,000+ orders**

---

## Key Business Findings

### Delivery Performance

- More than half of orders failed to meet expected delivery timelines
- Late delivery rate was approximately **55%**
- Most delays were short but occurred at very high volume

---

### Profitability Insights

Order profitability breakdown:

- Profitable Orders: **80.6%**
- Loss-Making Orders: **18.7%**
- Break-even Orders: **0.7%**

The biggest financial risk comes from repeated operational inefficiencies.

---

### Operational Bottlenecks

Major delay drivers discovered:

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| First Class Shipping | 100% delay rate |
| Second Class Shipping | ~80% delay rate |
| Highest Delay Region | Central Africa |
| High Risk Departments | Health & Beauty, Pet Shop |

Premium shipping modes were the biggest operational failure point.

---

## Root Cause Analysis

The main causes behind late deliveries were:

1. Inefficient premium shipping operations
2. Payment processing delays
3. Fulfillment capacity issues
4. Department-level operational constraints

---

## Predictive Analysis

A machine learning model was developed to identify orders at risk of late delivery.

Model Performance:

- Accuracy: 74%
- Late delivery prediction precision: 78%

This enables proactive intervention before delivery failures occur.

---

## Recommendations

Recommended business actions:

- Audit First Class and Second Class shipping capacity
- Improve carrier performance monitoring
- Optimize payment approval processes
- Adjust delivery promise calculations
- Build seasonal fulfillment capacity plans
- Deploy predictive delay alert system
